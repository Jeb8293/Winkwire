Returns & Refunds

This policy explains when a return may be requested, the condition required for returned merchandise, and how refunds are handled.

Last updated: October 9, 2026

30-day return request period

Unless a product page states otherwise, you may request a return within 30 days after the item is delivered. To start a return, contact WinkWire@winkwire.com with your order number, the item you want to return, and the reason for the request.

Return condition

To be eligible for a standard return, merchandise should be in substantially the same condition in which it was received, with included accessories, manuals, components, and original packaging when reasonably available. Items showing misuse, unauthorized modification, abuse, missing major components, or damage caused after delivery may be denied or subject to a reduced refund where permitted by law.

Do not send returns without instructions

Please contact us before mailing a return. Return instructions and the appropriate return destination may vary by product. Merchandise sent to an incorrect address or without sufficient identifying information may delay processing.

Damaged, defective, or incorrect items

Please inspect your order promptly after delivery. If an item arrives damaged, appears defective, or is not the item ordered, contact us as soon as possible and include photos when helpful. For an approved claim involving our fulfillment error or an item that arrived damaged or defective, we will provide an appropriate resolution, which may include replacement, return instructions, or refund.

Return shipping

For ordinary preference-based returns, the customer is generally responsible for return shipping unless we agree otherwise. If the return is approved because the wrong item was sent or the item arrived damaged or defective, Wink Wire Electronics will provide instructions regarding return shipping.

Refund timing

After an approved return is received and inspected, we will notify you of the refund decision. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Financial institutions may require additional time to post a refund; a typical posting period is approximately 5–10 business days after the refund is issued.

Non-returnable items

Items specifically marked final sale, gift cards, downloadable or digital goods, and products that cannot be safely resold for hygiene or safety reasons may be non-returnable where permitted by law. Any product-specific exception will be disclosed before purchase when applicable.

Questions

For return or refund assistance, email WinkWire@winkwire.com or call (714) 788-9744.
