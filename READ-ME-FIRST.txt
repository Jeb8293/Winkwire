CRITICAL MERCHANT CENTER NOTE
Your store is a custom GitHub Pages front end connected to Shopify. Google Merchant Center product links frequently use Shopify-style URLs such as:
https://winkwireelectronics.com/products/product-handle

GitHub Pages cannot natively serve a real HTTP 200 page for an unlimited dynamic /products/{handle} route. This package adds a browser redirect from those routes to:
https://winkwireelectronics.com/product.html?handle=product-handle

That improves the shopper experience, but Google may still treat the original /products/{handle} request as an HTTP 404 before the redirect executes.

Before requesting another Merchant Center review, inspect several disapproved items in Merchant Center and look at their “Landing page” URLs. If they use /products/{handle}, the strongest fix is either:
A) have Shopify host winkwireelectronics.com so Shopify can serve /products/{handle} directly, OR
B) change the product landing URLs in the feed to the 200-status product.html?handle=... format.

Do not request a new Google review until the live pages and checkout have been tested.
