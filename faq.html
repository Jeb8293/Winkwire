Frequently asked questions

Answers before you have to ask.

These answers explain how the storefront works. Order-specific timing, eligibility, and charges are governed by the policies linked below and the information shown at checkout.

Is WinkWire a physical retail store?
No. Winkwire Electronics & More is an online retailer. The Saint Augustine address shown on the website is the business/mailing address and is not a walk-in storefront.
How do I know the price I see is current?
Product prices and availability on the catalog and product pages are requested from the connected Shopify store. The final amount, including any applicable taxes, shipping charges, and discounts, is shown in Shopify checkout before you place the order.
How do I pay?
WinkWire does not collect card details in a custom form. When you are ready to purchase, the cart sends you to the store's Shopify checkout. The payment methods available for your order are displayed there.
What is your shipping time?
Please read the current Shipping Policy. Shipping terms can vary with the store's active fulfillment arrangements, so we publish the current policy rather than making a blanket promise here.
Can I return an item?
Return eligibility, timeframes, condition requirements, return-shipping responsibility, and refund processing are stated in the current Returns & Refunds Policy.
How do I get help with an order?
Email Winkwire@winkwire.com or call (714) 788-9744. Include your order number when possible so the order can be identified quickly.
Why do some products have several options?
Some Shopify products have variants such as size, color, capacity, or style. Choose the specific variant on the product page before adding it to your cart. Price and availability can differ by variant.
