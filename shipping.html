Shipping Policy

This policy explains order processing, shipping charges, delivery estimates, tracking, and what to do if a shipment has a problem.

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Order processing

Orders are generally processed within 1–3 business days after payment is confirmed, excluding weekends and major holidays. Some items may require additional handling time. If a product has an unusual lead time, we will make reasonable efforts to disclose it on the product page, at checkout, or in an order communication.

Shipping destinations and charges

Available shipping destinations and shipping charges are shown during Shopify checkout before you submit payment. We do not intentionally add undisclosed shipping charges after an order is placed.

Estimated delivery time

For U.S. orders, standard delivery commonly takes approximately 3–10 business days after processing, depending on destination, carrier, and product. Delivery estimates are not guarantees and may be affected by carrier delays, weather, holidays, address issues, or other circumstances outside our control.

Tracking

When tracking is available, it will be sent using the contact information provided with the order. Tracking may take time to update after a shipping label is created.

Incorrect addresses

Please review your shipping address carefully before completing checkout. If you notice an error, contact us as soon as possible at WinkWire@winkwire.com. We cannot guarantee that an address can be changed after an order enters fulfillment.

Lost, delayed, or damaged shipments

If tracking shows an unusual delay, a delivery issue, or your package arrives damaged, contact us with your order number and relevant photos or carrier information. We will review the issue and help determine the appropriate next step.

Questions

For shipping questions, email WinkWire@winkwire.com or call (714) 788-9744.
