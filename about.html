About WinkWire

We built the store we wanted to shop from.

Simple product information, useful merchandise, clear policies, secure checkout, and a real human way to ask for help.

Dear Customer,

Thank you for visiting Winkwire Electronics & More.

We started WinkWire because we believe online shopping should be simple. You should not have to dig through confusing listings, wonder whether a product will actually fit your needs, or hunt for a way to contact the business after you place an order. We wanted to build a store we would trust ourselves, and that is what we work toward every day.

Our name comes from a simple idea: technology and everyday products should work quickly, clearly, and without unnecessary friction. Electronics remain at the heart of the brand, but “& More” gives us room to carry practical finds outside traditional tech when they genuinely earn a place in the catalog.

What you can expect from us

We work to select products for usefulness and value, keep descriptions grounded in the information supplied for the actual item, and keep pricing and availability synchronized with Shopify. We do not use our website to hide the terms of a purchase. Customer-service contact information and the store's shipping, returns, privacy, and terms pages remain accessible from every page.

Checkout is handled through Shopify. That means payment information is entered on Shopify's secure checkout rather than into a home-built payment form on this website.

A small online business, not a mystery seller

Winkwire Electronics & More is an online retailer. Our business/mailing address is 11 8th St, Saint Augustine, FL 32080; it is not a walk-in retail location. If you need help with a product or order, email Winkwire@winkwire.com or call (714) 788-9744.

Thank you for choosing WinkWire. We appreciate the opportunity to earn your business.

Warm regards,
BG
Founder, Winkwire Electronics & More
