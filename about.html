ABOUT WINK WIRE

Simple shopping. Clear information.

Wink Wire Electronics is the storefront operated at winkwireelectronics.com. We focus on practical electronics and accessories, secure Shopify checkout, and customer support that is easy to find.

Dear Customer,

Thank you for visiting Wink Wire Electronics.

We started Wink Wire because we believe buying electronics should be simple. You should not have to dig through confusing listings, wonder how to get help after an order, or search for the policies that apply to your purchase. Our goal is to create a store we would feel comfortable buying from ourselves.

Our name comes from a simple idea: technology should work as quickly and easily as a wink. Whether you need a charger, an accessory, a gadget for your home, or something for your workspace, we look for products that are practical, useful, and worth considering.

Here is what you can expect from us:

  • Clear product information. Product titles, prices, availability, and descriptions are displayed before checkout.
  • Published customer policies. Shipping, returns and refunds, privacy, and terms are linked throughout the site.
  • Real contact information. Customers can reach Wink Wire Electronics by email at WinkWire@winkwire.com or by phone at (714) 788-9744.
  • Secure checkout. Orders are completed through Shopify's commerce platform.

We are a small, independent online store, and earning customer trust matters to us. If something is unclear, please contact us before placing an order and we will be glad to help.

Thank you for choosing Wink Wire Electronics. We are glad you are here.

Warm regards,
BG
