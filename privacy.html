Privacy Policy

This policy describes the information that may be handled when you browse Wink Wire Electronics, contact us, or complete a purchase through Shopify.

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Information you provide

We may receive information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, shipping or billing details, order information, and the contents of customer-support communications. Payment-card processing is handled through Shopify and its payment partners; this website does not intentionally store full payment-card numbers.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, technical information may be collected automatically, including browser type, device information, IP address, referring pages, pages visited, and basic usage information. The storefront also uses local browser storage to remember a Shopify cart identifier.

How information is used

Information may be used to provide and fulfill orders, process checkout, communicate about transactions, respond to support requests, prevent fraud or abuse, maintain site security, improve the storefront, and comply with legal obligations.

Service providers

We use service providers to operate the store. Shopify provides commerce, cart, checkout, and related ecommerce services. Hosting, domain, analytics, communications, payment, shipping, or fraud-prevention providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.

Cookies and similar technologies

Shopify and other service providers may use cookies or similar technologies for essential commerce functions, security, preferences, analytics, or other permitted purposes. Browser settings may allow you to control certain cookies, but disabling necessary technologies can affect site functionality.

Sale or sharing of personal information

Wink Wire Electronics does not sell personal information for money. Some privacy laws use broader definitions of “sale” or “sharing” that can include certain advertising or analytics disclosures. Where applicable, requests regarding these rights may be sent to our contact email.

Data retention and security

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for business, customer-service, accounting, legal, fraud-prevention, and operational purposes. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure, but reasonable safeguards are used by the services supporting the store.

Your privacy choices

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights to request access, correction, deletion, or other treatment of personal information. To make a request, email WinkWire@winkwire.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.
