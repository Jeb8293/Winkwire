FAQ

Are the products shown here live inventory?

When the storefront is configured, titles, images, prices, variants, and availability come directly from Shopify. If Shopify is not configured, Winkwire enters a clearly labeled preview state and purchasing is disabled.

Where does payment happen?

Checkout is handled by Shopify through the secure checkout URL returned for your cart. Winkwire does not collect card information on this site.

Where can I find shipping and return information?

Read the current Shipping Policy and Returns & Refunds Policy. These pages load the policies published by Winkwire in Shopify so the storefront and checkout information remain aligned.
